## Other active assets

- Fraunces 600 and DM Sans 400/500/600 come from Fontsource 5.3.0 with complete SIL OFL notices. The prior audit matched source bytes and notices.
- Camera and Sparkle paths come from Phosphor React 2.1.10, with its complete MIT notice, copyright 2020 Phosphor Icons.
- Pastel Purple uses independently authored AI-assisted SVG geometry, rasterized locally. The current paper/border replaced the earlier Cream Moonwalk and decorated Pastel Purple candidates; both strip footers now use the existing minimalist logo. Keep the editable SVG alongside the PNG.
- The active white placeholder was generated from opaque-white constants, without reading the earlier image. Its generator is retained at [tools/generate-placeholder.js](../tools/generate-placeholder.js), and its identity is recorded in the asset notes.

The prior audit recorded no identified missing third-party grant for the active inventory. Retain the license notices. Recorded generation provenance and owner intent do not establish exclusivity, trademark clearance or rights in any independently protected reference material. Historical terms analysis and detailed audit records remain in Git at `de170d5`; this summary does not update or reinterpret those terms.
